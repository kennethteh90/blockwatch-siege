The pitch

A tower defense game,
made entirely of commands.

No mods. No client downloads. Just drop the datapack into your world's datapacks/ folder, run /reload, and start a match. Your friends only need a vanilla 1.20.4 client.

01 / BUILD

Place towers in 30 seconds.

Eight build pads sit along two parallel lanes. Stand on a pad, run /trigger bw.place 1 — Arrow, Frost, or Bomb. Plan your kill zones before the first wave marches.

02 / DEFEND

Towers fire automatically.

Combat is hands-off. Watch your placements light up enemies as they teleport down the lanes. Frost slows runners, Bomb shreds clusters, Arrow does the steady work.

03 / ESCALATE

Ten waves. One boss.

Earn gold each wave (more if you don't leak). Sell underperformers, reposition for dual-lane waves at 7+, and brace for the ravager that drops on wave 10.

Tower roster

Three towers.
Mix them well.

Every tower fits a role. Click any card to read where it shines and where it falls flat.

Arrow Tower

cost 50g · sell 35g · trigger 1
range8 blk damage5 hp rate1.0 s targetsingle

Best for: early waves where every gold piece counts, and as steady DPS through the late game. Cheap enough to stack two side-by-side on a pad pair.

Watch out for: dense clusters — Arrow only hits one target per shot, so packs of Walkers will leak past a single tower.

"The workhorse. Build two early, keep them, never apologize."

Tier 1 pick details

Frost Tower

cost 75g · sell 52g · trigger 2
range6 blk damage3 hp rate1.25 s freeze3.0 s

Best for: neutralizing Runners, who are otherwise fast enough to slip past Arrow towers. A frozen enemy stops dead and waits for your other towers to delete it.

Watch out for: low damage and short range — never run a lane on Frost alone. Pair it with Bomb behind, Arrow alongside.

"Frost makes everything else hit harder. It's force multiplier, not damage dealer."

Crowd control details

Bomb Tower

cost 100g · sell 70g · trigger 3
range10 blk damage15 hp aoe rate3.0 s targetall in range

Best for: dual-lane waves (7–10) and Brute clusters. One Bomb between the lanes can hit both at once. Bursts down the boss when paired with Frost lock-in.

Watch out for: the 3-second reload. Solo Bombs get overrun between shots — always sandwich with Arrow for sustain.

"Place it dead-center and watch a wave evaporate. Never solo it."

Hostile mobs

Know what's coming
down the lane.

Four enemy archetypes. The wave table mixes them in escalating combinations — by wave 7 you'll be defending both lanes at once.

WALKER
hp20 speed0.15 b/t first wave1 rolefodder

A slow, predictable zombie. Cheap to delete with Arrow towers — but they come in piles.

RUNNER
hp15 speed0.25 b/t first wave2 roleleaker

Cave spider. Fast enough to slip past slow towers if you don't have Frost backing you up.

BRUTE
hp100 speed0.08 b/t first wave3 roletank

Iron golem. Slow, but soaks five Arrow shots. Bomb burst is the answer.

BOSS
hp200 speed0.06 b/t first wave10 rolefinale

Ravager. One per match, lane 1, wave 10. Frost lock + double Bomb is the standard kill.
